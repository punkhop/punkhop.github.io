Privacy policy

Jetpack Comedy, West Hollywood. Last updated November 3, 2026.

What this covers

This policy covers jetpackcomedy.com and the ads Jetpack Comedy runs on Meta (Facebook and Instagram). Tickets are sold by Micdrop at get-micdrop.com, which has its own privacy policy covering your order, payment and email.

What we collect on this site

  • Visit measurement. The site uses Google Analytics 4 and the Meta Pixel. They record pages viewed, clicks on Get tickets, your approximate location, the device and browser you use, and set cookies to tell repeat visits apart. The Meta Pixel also lets us show ads to people who visited the site and measure whether an ad led to a ticket purchase.
  • Nothing you type. This site has no forms. Your name, email and payment details go to Micdrop and Stripe when you buy a ticket, never to this site.

What we do with it

We use these numbers to see which shows and ads bring people in and to reach people who have shown interest in the show. We do not sell your information. Google and Meta process it under their own terms: Google, Meta.

Your choices

  • Block or clear cookies in your browser to stop the measurement tags.
  • Control the ads you see from Meta in your Facebook ad preferences.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on.

Deleting your data

Email shows@jetpackcomedy.com and we will delete anything we hold about you and confirm when it is done. For ticket orders, contact Micdrop through the link in your order email.
